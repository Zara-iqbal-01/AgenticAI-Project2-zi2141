Draw Luck

How much did the draw decide a tennis Grand Slam?

What it exploresEvery men's and women's Grand Slam draw from 2015 to 2026 Roland Garros. Each draw is played 20,000 times with pre-tournament surface Elo, then compared with 20,000 random draws under the same seeding rules. The difference is the player's draw luck.
Who it's forTennis fans, journalists and analysts who want to know whether the draw robbed a favorite, which quarter was the group of death, and which dangerous players the rankings hid.
How to use itAsk about a specific Slam ("Wimbledon 2024 men's") or a pattern across Slams. A Lead agent sends researchers out in parallel, and a Writer cites every claim. Vague questions get a clarifying question back. Answers take 1–3 minutes.
